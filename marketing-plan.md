# AskMy.pink Marketing Plan

## Positioning
"Ask your people. Let AI do the research."

The easiest way for couples and families to make decisions together — with an AI that knows their habits and can help book it.

## Target Channels
1. **App Stores** — Strong ASO around "ask my wife", "family decisions", "AI recommendations for couples"
2. **Social Media** — TikTok, Instagram Reels, X showing real couple examples ("@husband where should we eat tonight?" → AI magic)
3. **Influencers** — Couple/family lifestyle creators, parenting accounts
4. **Word-of-mouth / Viral tagging** — The core mechanic is inherently shareable and viral within relationships
5. **Partnerships** — Restaurants, travel, event platforms for co-marketing and affiliate revenue
6. **Content** — Blog/SEO on askmy.pink about decision-making, relationship tips, habit insights

## Launch Strategy
- Soft launch with waitlist on https://askmy.pink
- Invite-only beta focused on couples
- Early access rewards for tagging and completing decisions
- Press / Product Hunt / App Store features highlighting the unique "tag + AI research" flow

## Messaging Pillars
- Natural: Feels like how you already ask your partner or friend
- Smarter: AI does the boring research and learns preferences
- Actionable: From question to reservation in one flow
- Private & relational: Centered on your people, not public social

## Metrics to Track
- Tags sent / received
- Decisions completed
- Bookings driven
- Habit learning accuracy / user retention
- Viral coefficient within households

## Always Notify
All major updates, launches, and milestones should include Slack notifications (e.g. #all-techmark or dedicated channel).
