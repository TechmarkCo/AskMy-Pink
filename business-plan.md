# AskMy.pink Business Plan

## Vision
Empower everyday decision-making in relationships and families by combining natural human consultation (tagging a trusted person) with AI-powered research, recommendations, and actionability. Make it as easy as asking your spouse or friend, but smarter and faster — especially on mobile.

## Problem
People constantly defer decisions to partners or friends ("What do you want for dinner?", "Should we book this?"). Current tools (group chats, search engines, separate booking apps) are fragmented, lack personalization, and don't learn shared habits.

## Solution
A mobile app where:
1. User tags a contact with a natural language question (voice or text).
2. AI immediately researches relevant options based on location, time, known preferences, and learned habits of both parties.
3. Delivers a rich chat message to the tagged person containing the original ask + AI recommendations + action buttons (reserve, book, save, etc.).
4. Continuous learning of individual and joint habits for better future suggestions.

## Target Users
- Couples and partners
- Families (parents, kids, siblings)
- Close friend groups
- Primary: ages 25–45, urban/suburban, smartphone-native, value convenience

## Monetization
- Freemium: basic tagging + AI recs free; premium for advanced bookings, priority notifications, habit insights, family plans
- Affiliate / partnership commissions on reservations and bookings (restaurants, travel, events, services)
- Optional white-label or B2B for larger family groups / organizations

## Competitive Advantage
- Focuses on the *relational* layer (asking someone) rather than pure AI search or pure social
- Deep personalization via habit learning across tagged relationships
- Voice-first + instant notifications designed for real-life, on-the-go use
- Actionable AI (not just suggestions, but path to booking)
