## The Idea
This is a new social network platform with AI. The concept is that people usually ask their spouse or friends about something when making a decision. This platform allows you to tag someone to ask a question and it gets passed to that person as a chat message but with AI suggestions.

**Example:**  
Husband asks wife: "@wife where do you want to go eat around main street".  
The AI will help do some research and pass both the message and recommendations to the wife to answer. The AI will have recommendations with possibility of AI reservations or booking or other suggestions.

This is normally how family and couples relate, by asking each other before making a decision, but now with an AI assistant.

## Core Experience
- Tag a trusted person (`@wife`, `@husband`, `@friend`, family member) with a natural-language question.
- The tagged person receives it as a chat message enriched by AI.
- AI performs research (location, options, preferences) and adds recommendations.
- AI can suggest or assist with reservations, bookings, or other actions.
- The platform learns the habits of the people who use it so future recommendations get better for individuals and for the relationships between them.

## Design Principles
- **Mobile-first / on-the-go**: Must be a mobile app for easy use anywhere.
- **Voice + notifications**: Easy voice input and clear, timely notifications so it feels as natural as texting or calling your partner.
- **Relational, not public**: Focused on private family and couple decision-making rather than broad social broadcasting.
- **Actionable AI**: Not just suggestions — path toward booking or deciding.
- **Habit-aware**: Continuously learns preferences and patterns of the people involved.

## How AI Assists
1. Parses the tagged question and intent.
2. Researches relevant options (restaurants, activities, products, travel, etc.).
3. Personalizes using known habits of the asker and the tagged person.
4. Delivers the original message + researched recommendations in one chat.
5. Offers next-step actions (reserve, book, save, compare).
6. Improves over time from accepted decisions and feedback.
